# Continuous Monitoring Strategy

## Overview

Following authorization, the HRIS enters the Continuous Monitoring phase of the NIST Risk Management Framework (RMF). Continuous monitoring ensures that security controls remain effective and that new risks are identified and addressed throughout the operational lifecycle of the system.

The objective of continuous monitoring is to maintain ongoing awareness of the system's security posture, support risk based decision making, and ensure compliance with federal security requirements.

The continuous monitoring strategy for the HRIS focuses on access management, vulnerability management, system logging, configuration monitoring, and ongoing risk management activities.

---

## Access Control Monitoring

User access to the HRIS is periodically reviewed to ensure permissions remain aligned with job responsibilities and organizational policies.

Monitoring activities include:

- Quarterly user access reviews
- Validation of role based access assignments
- Removal of inactive or unnecessary accounts
- Review of privileged access permissions

---

## Vulnerability Management

The HRIS environment is periodically evaluated for vulnerabilities that may impact system security.

Because the HRIS operates as a SaaS solution, vulnerability management responsibilities are shared between the vendor and the organization.

Monitoring activities include:

- Review of vendor security advisories and vulnerability disclosures
- Monitoring SaaS vendor patching and maintenance notifications
- Tracking remediation efforts for identified weaknesses through the POA&M
- Coordination with the vendor when security vulnerabilities affect the HRIS tenant environment

---

## Security Log Monitoring

System and authentication logs are reviewed to detect potential security incidents or suspicious activity.

Monitoring activities include:

- Monitoring authentication and system access logs
- Investigation of unusual login patterns or system activity
- Review of administrative activity within the HRIS platform
- Integration with organizational monitoring and alerting capabilities when available

---

## Configuration and Change Monitoring

Changes to HRIS configurations, integrations, or system settings are reviewed to ensure that security controls remain properly implemented.

Monitoring activities include:

- Review of system configuration changes
- Validation that security controls remain properly implemented
- Documentation updates when system components or integrations change
- Review of vendor platform updates that may affect security configurations

---

## POA&M Management

Security weaknesses identified during assessments, monitoring activities, or audits are documented in the Plan of Action and Milestones (POA&M).

POA&M items are tracked until remediation is completed or risk acceptance is formally approved. Responsible parties and remediation timelines are documented and periodically reviewed.

---

## Periodic Security Assessments

Selected security controls may be reassessed periodically to verify continued effectiveness.

Periodic assessments support ongoing risk management and help identify potential gaps in control implementation.

---

## Continuous Monitoring Outcome

Continuous monitoring supports ongoing risk management and ensures that the HRIS system maintains an acceptable security posture throughout its operational lifecycle.
