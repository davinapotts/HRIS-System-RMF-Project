# FIPS 199 Security Categorization Analysis

## Information System Categorization

Security categorization in accordance with FIPS 199:

{(Confidentiality, Moderate), (Integrity, Moderate), (Availability, Moderate)}

Overall System Security Impact Level: Moderate

---

## Confidentiality Impact Level: Moderate

Unauthorized disclosure of employee PII, payroll data, or benefits information could result in identity theft, financial harm to individuals, reputational damage to the agency, and regulatory consequences under federal privacy requirements. The adverse effect would be serious but not catastrophic.

---

## Integrity Impact Level: Moderate

Unauthorized modification or corruption of HR data, including payroll, employment status, or benefits records, could result in improper compensation, legal disputes, operational disruption, and loss of trust in agency administrative functions. The adverse effect would be serious but not catastrophic.

---

## Availability Impact Level: Moderate

Loss of system availability would disrupt payroll processing, benefits administration, and HR operations. Although short-term outages may be manageable through manual processes, extended downtime would significantly affect agency operations and employee services. The adverse effect would be serious but not catastrophic.

---

## Impact if the System Failed

- Payroll disruption, including delayed or inaccurate employee compensation  
- Errors in benefits administration and employment records  
- Increased manual processing and higher administrative burden  
- Loss of data availability affecting HR operations and employee self-service  
- Compliance violations related to payroll, tax reporting, or employee data protection  
- Financial loss and potential legal exposure  
- Reputational damage and reduced employee trust in organizational systems
